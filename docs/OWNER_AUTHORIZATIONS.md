# Owner Standing Authorizations

Version: 1.0  
Confirmed by project owner: 2026-09-30

This file records standing authorizations for Codex/browser execution in the NHÀ MỸ CALI web-presence project.

## 1. Scope

The authorized project owner grants Codex standing permission to perform routine account-registration and profile/publication setup actions on platforms that are already approved by this repository.

This authorization is intended to reduce repeated action-time prompts for ordinary signup work.

## 2. Standard terms acceptance — pre-approved

For an approved **free account, free profile, free publication, or free channel** workflow, Codex is pre-authorized to click or select ordinary acceptance controls such as:

- I agree;
- Accept;
- Agree and continue;
- Continue;
- Create account;
- Create publication;
- Publisher Agreement acceptance;
- Terms of Service acceptance;
- Privacy Policy acknowledgement;
- Community Guidelines acknowledgement;
- cookie/privacy choices required only to continue ordinary account setup;
- equivalent standard registration-consent controls.

Codex does **not** need to ask the owner again solely because the platform requires acceptance of its standard publicly presented Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Community Guidelines, or standard free Publisher/Creator Agreement.

This authorization applies when:
- the platform/workflow is approved in the repo;
- the action is part of ordinary account/profile/publication creation or setup;
- no payment or financial account is being created;
- no unsupported factual representation is required;
- the action does not bypass a platform security control.

## 3. Truthful-attestation boundary

Standing authorization to accept standard terms does **not** authorize Codex to invent or falsely attest facts.

If a checkbox or agreement requires a factual representation such as:
- exact age or date of birth;
- legal identity;
- business registration status;
- authority to bind a separate legal entity;
- residency/citizenship;
- tax status;
- professional license not already canonical;
- ownership of content/assets not already established;
- government-ID facts;

Codex may proceed only when the relevant fact is already supported by canonical project data or an explicit owner instruction.

If the fact is missing or uncertain, stop only for that missing fact.

## 4. Commercial / financial boundary

This standing authorization does not include:
- purchasing a subscription;
- accepting paid-plan pricing;
- starting a paid trial that will charge automatically;
- connecting Stripe;
- connecting a card or bank account;
- purchasing a custom domain;
- buying ads/credits;
- accepting a financial commitment.

Those actions require separate explicit authorization for the exact transaction.

## 5. Security boundary

This standing authorization does not authorize bypassing:
- CAPTCHA or anti-bot controls;
- OTP/2FA/security challenges;
- identity-document verification;
- access controls;
- account-recovery protections.

Codex may use normal platform controls and owner-approved credentials, but must not defeat the platform's security mechanisms.

## 6. Terms/policy conflicts

Do not stop merely because a normal signup page presents standard Terms/Privacy/Community/Publisher agreements.

Stop only when the live terms or policy:
- expressly prohibit the intended automation/workflow;
- require a non-routine legal/commercial commitment outside the authorization above;
- require an unsupported factual/legal attestation;
- conflict materially with repository policy.

## 7. Product-level approvals

Repository authorization cannot override mandatory approval prompts enforced by Codex, ChatGPT, the browser, the workspace, or the external platform.

If the product itself requires an action-time approval, request/obtain that approval once and continue without treating it as a project-policy blocker.

## 8. Execution rule

Within the scope above, Codex should behave as an authorized operator for NHÀ MỸ CALI:

- do not ask for confirmation before each standard acceptance checkbox/button;
- accept standard free-account terms and continue;
- finish the approved workflow;
- record only non-secret execution state;
- stop only at the specific boundaries listed above.

This is a standing project authorization until the owner revokes or replaces it.
