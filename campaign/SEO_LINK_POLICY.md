# SEO & Link Policy — NHÀ MỸ CALI Web Presence

Version: 1.0  
Updated: 2026-09-28

## 1. Principle

This project does **not** optimize for the number of backlinks created.

The preferred outcome is:

> **credible entity presence + useful distribution + relevant referral traffic + naturally earned links**

A link is not automatically valuable because it exists.

## 2. Acceptable link patterns

Examples:

- official website field in a legitimate brand profile;
- author/profile link on a platform where NHÀ MỸ CALI genuinely publishes;
- contextual link from a useful article to a directly relevant resource;
- RSS/feed discovery;
- citations from organizations, publications, partners, directories, or community resources where the mention is legitimate;
- branded links such as `NHÀ MỸ CALI`, `nhamycali.com`, or the naked canonical URL.

## 3. Prohibited campaign behavior

Do not automate:

- forum-comment backlinks;
- repetitive Reddit self-promotion;
- blog-comment links;
- exact-match anchor campaigns;
- mass bookmarking for ranking manipulation;
- spun articles;
- duplicate thin articles across many domains;
- fake user-generated discussions;
- fake reviews;
- fake local citations;
- purchased editorial links without appropriate disclosure/qualification;
- link exchanges created primarily to manipulate ranking;
- automated account farms.

## 4. Anchor-text policy

Default profile/brand anchors:

- NHÀ MỸ CALI
- Nha My Cali
- nhamycali.com
- https://nhamycali.com/

Contextual descriptive anchors are allowed when natural, for example:

- cẩm nang mua nhà tại Mỹ
- hướng dẫn người mua nhà lần đầu
- nhà đang bán tại San Jose

But do not repeat one exact commercial anchor across many third-party platforms.

## 5. Destination policy

Prefer linking to the most relevant destination.

Examples:

- general brand profile → homepage;
- first-time buyer article → relevant buyer guide;
- Open House content → current verified Open House/listing page;
- local market article → current relevant market resource.

Do not force every link to the homepage if a deeper page is more useful.

## 6. Publishing-platform rule

Blogger, WordPress.com, Medium, Substack, Tumblr, Google Sites, and similar platforms must not become clones of the main site.

Allowed:

- original platform-native article;
- substantial adaptation with different framing;
- excerpt/summary with a link to a deeper original;
- newsletter edition;
- curated resource page.

Avoid:

- full duplicate reposts at scale;
- search-keyword doorway pages;
- dozens of thin pages whose only purpose is a link.

## 7. RSS/distribution rule

Feed readers and aggregators are distribution tools, not a link-manufacturing trick.

Use them to:

- make the site's feed discoverable;
- follow/publish legitimate feed metadata;
- broaden content access.

Do not describe private subscriptions or saved feeds as “SEO backlinks” unless the resulting page is actually public, indexable, and independently useful.

## 8. Bookmark/note/productivity tools

Examples include:

- Evernote
- OneNote
- Dropbox
- Trello
- Toodledo
- Instapaper

These are not default SEO backlink targets.

Only create/use such accounts when they serve an operational purpose.

## 9. Community platforms

For Reddit, Disqus, and similar communities:

- no automated link drops;
- no repetitive promotional replies;
- no pretending to be an unrelated customer;
- disclose affiliation where community rules require it;
- contribute useful content before promotion.

Default campaign status: **manual/community plan required**.

## 10. Link attributes

Do not choose a platform based on “dofollow” promises.

Sites may use:

- `rel="nofollow"`
- `rel="ugc"`
- `rel="sponsored"`

That is normal.

Do not attempt to circumvent a platform's link qualification.

## 11. Entity consistency

SEO benefit also comes from consistent identity.

Keep stable where appropriate:

- brand name;
- website;
- phone;
- location;
- Helen credentials;
- official social URLs;
- description of the business.

Do not change facts just to diversify citations.

## 12. Quality test

Before creating a public link, ask:

> “Would this profile/page/content still be worth creating if search engines passed zero ranking credit through the link?”

If no:
- do not create it as part of this campaign.

## 13. Best long-term link opportunities

High-value targets to develop separately:

- Vietnamese community organizations in Bay Area;
- local business/community publications;
- real-estate interviews;
- expert quotes;
- neighborhood guides people cite;
- original local market research;
- event/community sponsorship pages;
- professional associations;
- legitimate partner/resource pages.

These require relationship/content work rather than mass signup automation.

## 14. Google policy references

Agents should re-check current guidance before scaling because policies change.

Reference starting points:

- Google Search spam policies:  
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

- Qualifying outbound links:  
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links

Do not assume this repository's summary overrides current search-engine policy.

## 15. Measurement

Do not measure success by raw backlink count alone.

Prefer:

- number of verified canonical profiles;
- referral visits;
- branded search growth;
- indexable useful content;
- mentions/citations;
- newsletter/feed subscribers;
- social discovery;
- qualified leads;
- earned editorial links;
- absence of spam/suspension issues.
