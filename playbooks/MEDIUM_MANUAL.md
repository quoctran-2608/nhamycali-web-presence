# Medium Manual-Only Workflow

Research snapshot: 2026-09-28

## Hard rule

Under the current Medium Rules, do **not** have Codex/browser automation:

- register a Medium account;
- systematically/programmatically post content;
- automate interactions.

Codex may prepare content and a setup checklist. A human performs platform actions through the normal Medium UI.

## Strategic role

Medium can still be useful for:
- educational essays;
- first-time buyer explanations;
- real-estate process education;
- selected California/Bay Area commentary.

It should not be a duplicate archive of nhamycali.com.

## Manual QA checklist

Human should verify:
- public name;
- bio;
- profile image;
- website link;
- no private registration email;
- no unsupported claims;
- article has independent reader value.

## Repo state

Until a human actually creates and verifies the public profile:
- keep Medium as `manual_action_required`.

After the human provides/records the public URL:
- update state;
- update `brand/OFFICIAL_LINKS.yaml` if appropriate;
- do not falsely label the account as automation-created.
